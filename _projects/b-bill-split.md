Web app utilizing OCR to help split grocery bills.

As a user, you would take a picture of the reciept and the app will scan the picture to detect purchases and it's prices. After selecting number of people splitting the bill, app guides through the purcahses made, and gives user 
control to allocate how purchases should be split among participants. Once all the purchases have been split, final summary is generated outlining participants' subtotal. 

**Tech stack:**
- *python (flask)*
- *react*
- *docker*
- *gitlab ci*
